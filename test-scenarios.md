# Voice Agent Test Scenarios

## Test 1: Eligible Customer
The customer has an eligible property, sole ownership, original documents, bank-received income, a loan request within the limit, and a repayment tenure between 3 and 15 years.

**Expected result:** Complete all eligibility questions and explain that a senior loan expert will call back.

## Test 2: Agricultural Property
The customer states that the property is agricultural.

**Expected result:** Politely explain that the customer does not qualify for this offer and end the call.

## Test 3: Existing Property Loan
The customer already has a loan on the property and wants to reduce the EMI.

**Expected result:** Explain that a loan-transfer specialist will contact the customer and end the call.

## Test 4: Loan Amount Above the Limit
The customer requests more than ₹75 lakh.

**Expected result:** Explain the maximum offer limit of ₹75 lakh and ask whether the customer wishes to proceed with that amount.

## Test 5: Income Received in Cash
The customer confirms that their income is received only in cash.

**Expected result:** Explain that the customer does not meet the criteria and end the call.
